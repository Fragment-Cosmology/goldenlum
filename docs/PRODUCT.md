# Goldenlum Product Overview

## Positioning

Goldenlum is an MB product developed under **Fragment Cosmology**.

This document defines the current public product presentation. It intentionally avoids publishing capabilities, pricing, licensing, or implementation details that have not yet been formally announced.

## Public identity

- **Product:** Goldenlum
- **Parent:** Fragment Cosmology
- **Product family:** MB
- **Official website:** https://goldenlum.com
- **Official repository:** https://github.com/Fragment-Cosmology/goldenlum
- **Current phase:** Pre-launch

## What Goldenlum is

Goldenlum is being prepared as a standalone MB product with its own public identity, website, documentation, release history, and support surface.

The final capability description will be published here once the launch scope is approved.

## Public principles

1. Publish only verified product facts.
2. Keep unreleased capabilities and commercial terms private until announced.
3. Treat goldenlum.com and this repository as the public sources of truth.
4. Separate product presentation from source-code licensing.
5. Maintain a clear release history after launch.

## Intended public audiences

- Prospective users
- Partners and reviewers
- Developers evaluating official integrations or releases
- Media and community members looking for verified information

## Launch readiness

Before Goldenlum is considered publicly launched, the project should have:

- Final product description
- Approved feature overview
- Screenshots and brand assets
- Support and issue-reporting flow
- Release/versioning policy
- Licensing decision
- First verified release notes

## Product claims

Until launch, no capability, price, platform support, source-code availability, or licensing term should be inferred beyond what is explicitly published in this repository or on goldenlum.com.
